## 5. L1 vs L2 Differentiation Analysis

- **Level 1 Characteristics**: Broad vocational discovery, exploratory interest preferences (*"Which activity appeals to you?"*), subjective motivation drivers.
- **Level 2 Characteristics**: Active cognitive operations (*"What valid deduction must follow?"*, *"Calculate the reorder point"*, *"Why is Method B superior in logarithmic complexity?"*, *"Identify the sampling bias"*).
- **Verification**: Zero Level 2 questions use passive preference queries. All 37 questions engage structured analytical deduction, mathematical formulas, hypothesis control, or business decision logic.
