## 5. L1–L5 Longitudinal Layering & Differentiation Analysis

- **Level 1 (DISCOVER)**: Broad vocational discovery and exploratory interests (*"Which activity appeals to you?"*).
- **Level 2 (REASON)**: Foundational logic, deduction, and quantitative rule confirmation (*"What valid deduction must follow?"*).
- **Level 3 (APPLY)**: Practical situational judgment, troubleshooting, and execution (*"Database CPU at 100%—what architectural remedy?"*).
- **Level 4 (DISCRIMINATE)**: Sharp branch-level pairwise forced-choice trade-offs (*"Quantum algebraic proofs (QR) vs cryogenic decoherence (SC)?"*).
- **Level 5 (VALIDATE)**: Complex multi-disciplinary capstone synthesis (*"In a $10B clean energy overhaul, do you own the macroeconomic clearing model (AR), the private debt underwriting (BU), the power grid engineering (PS), or the labor transition (SO)?"*).
- **Verification**: Zero Level 5 questions use narrow formula drills or passive interest queries. 100% require multi-stakeholder strategic role ownership under real-world constraints.
