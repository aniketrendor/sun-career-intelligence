# Stage 1 Adaptive Assessment Question Banks

## UG Bank

# Stage 1 UG Adaptive Question Bank

This is the undergraduate question pool for Stage 1. It contains 110 questions across five levels. Only 30 are presented to a student, with 6 selected from each level.

## Dimension codes

| Code | Dimension |
|---|---|
| AR | Analytical Reasoning |
| LR | Logical Reasoning |
| QR | Quantitative Reasoning |
| PS | Problem Solving |
| SC | Scientific Thinking |
| RE | Research Orientation |
| TC | Technology Orientation |
| CR | Creativity |
| CO | Communication |
| SO | Social Orientation |
| LE | Leadership & Management |
| BU | Business Orientation |

## Assessment structure

| Level | Questions in bank | Questions presented |
|---|---:|---:|
| Level 1 | 15 | 6 |
| Level 2 | 20 | 6 |
| Level 3 | 25 | 6 |
| Level 4 | 25 | 6 |
| Level 5 | 25 | 6 |
| **Total** | **110** | **30** |

The bank is adaptive. The student receives 6 questions at each level. The system selects those 6 from the level pool based on the student's current profile. Option positions should be randomized in the application.

This is the postgraduate question pool for Stage 1. It contains 110 questions across five levels. Only 30 are presented to a student, with 6 selected from each level. PG questions assume prior undergraduate exposure and use more advanced research, analytical and professional contexts.

| Code | Dimension |
|---|---|

| QR | Quantitative Reasoning |

| RE | Research Orientation |
| TC | Technology Orientation |
| CR | Creativity |
| CO | Communication |
| SO | Social Orientation |
| LE | Leadership & Management |
| BU | Business Orientation |

| Level | Questions in bank | Questions presented |
|---|---:|---:|
| Level 1 | 15 | 6 |
| Level 2 | 20 | 6 |
| Level 3 | 25 | 6 |
| Level 4 | 25 | 6 |
| Level 5 | 25 | 6 |
| **Total** | **110** | **30** |
